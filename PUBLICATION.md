Public documentation omits private résumé comparisons and account/billing context. Home-directory prefixes in historical logs are redacted. The account-specific provisioning helper and its two tests are excluded; the product CPU suite has 38 tests, while the historical 40-test total includes those two helper tests. Original source manifests, source-bundle records and reviewer attestations describe their original accepted workspace.
